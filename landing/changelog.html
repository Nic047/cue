The latest improvements to cue.

v0.1.8 Latest · AlphaOctober 5, 2026

Better with a second screen.

cue stays on your MacBook’s screen, recent chats are simpler, and updates happen in the background.

On the right screen

  • The pill and results panel stay on the built-in MacBook display, even when your cursor is on an external monitor.
  • When the built-in display is unavailable, cue uses the main display.

Cleaner recent chats

  • Recent chats focus as soon as they open, so the close button works on the first click.
  • Removed the “Latest” label, trailing arrows, and visible scrollbar. You can still scroll through your chats.
  • Brought back a brighter text shimmer in the pill.

Updates without another window

  • cue checks for updates at launch and every six hours.
  • The menu only shows “One update available” when there is a new version. Click it to install.
  • Removed the separate update window and the manual check option from the menu.
  • Update availability is also shown in Settings.

A sharper installer

  • The drag-to-Applications window now uses Retina-resolution artwork.

v0.1.7 AlphaOctober 4, 2026

What’s new in cue

A few fixes and improvements in 0.1.7.

Panels that stay out of your way

  • Click outside an expanded panel to dismiss it.
  • Recent chats close when you switch apps or open another panel.
  • Improved overlay behavior when switching between apps.

More useful shortcuts

  • Double-tap your shortcut to open the latest result, or recent chats if there is no result yet.
  • Requests with no actionable task now clear the pill instead of leaving it waiting.

A more consistent interface

  • Smaller result panels, more balanced spacing, and cleaner recent chats.
  • Better alignment for shortcut and model controls in onboarding.
  • Refined layouts for Macs with and without a notch.

Updates, built in

  • Check for updates from the menu bar or Settings.
  • Future updates verify their signature before installation.

Already using 0.1.6 or earlier? Install 0.1.7 manually once to get the updater.
